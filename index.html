    🖨️ Print-prep tips (Fourthwall, black tee)
    
        
      
          
    1. AI images are smaller than 3600×4800 px. Gemini (Nano Banana 2 / Pro) maxes out at 3584×4800 at 4K, 3:4, which is 16 px too narrow, so pad or resample to 3600. Every Gemini image carries an invisible SynthID watermark. Grok Imagine outputs 1k or 2k, so upscale at least 2× for 300 DPI and about 4–5× for 600 DPI.
      2. 
          
    2. Neither generator outputs real transparency, even when the prompt asks for a "transparent PNG". Ask for a flat, pure-white (or solid) background outside the orange border, then remove it yourself. The Grok app (grok.com/imagine) has a built-in background-removal tool that exports a transparent PNG. The API does not.
      3. 
          
    3. Remove the background for free with rembg (code is MIT). ⚠️ Its default model (bria-rmbg / RMBG-2.0) needs a paid BRIA agreement for commercial use, so pick a permissive model instead, e.g. rembg i -m birefnet-general in.png out.png (or isnet-general-use / u2net), and check that model's weights license. Photopea's magic wand or GIMP flood-fill also works on a flat white background.
      4. 
          
    4. Upscale 2–4× with Upscayl (free, local, AGPL). For pixel art use nearest-neighbor instead, because AI upscalers smooth it. Then fit the canvas to 3600×4800 px at 300 DPI (or 7200×9600 at 600 DPI) and set the DPI metadata.
      5. 
          
    5. Kill halos: threshold the alpha channel and check edges at 100% zoom on a black preview. Leftover white fringe shows badly on black fabric.
      6. 
          
    6. Export PNG, sRGB, transparent, ≤200 MB. No JPG (no transparency, and it adds artifacts). Fourthwall's help pages disagree on CMYK vs sRGB and on 300 vs 200 DPI (see Trend Research → Fourthwall specs), so sRGB PNG at 300 DPI+ is the safe choice. Order a sample.
      7. 
          
    7. Black tees: avoid glows, soft gradients, and semi-transparency. Thick outlines and bright solid colors print best. Fourthwall's help pages say DTFx gives brighter results on dark shirts.
      8. 
          
    8. Text: AI often misspells. Proofread, then fix it with an edit pass or in Photopea/Inkscape. Keep text at least ~4 pt / 1 mm.
      9. 
          
    9. Legal: no trademarks, logos, characters, or celebrity likeness. Original art only (Fourthwall's Acceptable Use Policy).
